# LUNAD

**Book. Ride. Explore Catanduanes.** Book a Direct ride (car or van) or a TODA route (tricycle), see who is online, and pay the driver directly. Every operator is LGU-verified.

**Demo script**

1\. **User → Ride**: set pickup and drop-off, then request. The offer goes to the nearest online operator, who has 3 minutes to answer.

2\. Switch to **Operator** and accept, or decline to pass it to the next one. Then arrive → start → complete.

3\. Back as User: pay by QR, upload proof, rate, or report an issue.

4\. Message the operator directly from an active trip — no phone numbers are shown.

**Prototype notes**

All names, fares, permits and numbers are sample data. The map is stylized. SMS and Messenger are simulated in the Inbox. Data stays in this browser only.
